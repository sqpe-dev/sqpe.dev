# Historical research input

`state-of-the-art.md` is the original working survey used to discover sources. It is retained as an immutable input to the reference reconciliation, not as a reviewed publication or the authoritative bibliography. Its descriptions, metadata, and related-work judgments may be incomplete or superseded.

The maintained records and corrections live in the [public catalog](https://github.com/sqpe-dev/catalog). The website's `data/reference-inventory.json` maps every bibliography entry in the survey to a catalog record. Pending verification is stated explicitly in the catalog; importing a citation does not establish a completed review.

Submit new sources and corrections to the catalog. Do not silently edit the historical input to match later interpretations.
